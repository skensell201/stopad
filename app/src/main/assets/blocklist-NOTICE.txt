StopAd parses the hosts records, removes localhost/IP entries and duplicates,
normalizes domain names and ignores unsupported underscore names.
Matching also covers subdomains; 14 local StopAd rules are applied separately.
For the bundled revision and SHA-256 see ads-snapshot.properties.
Updates use the same fixed HTTPS source and retain the original downloaded bytes.
